Nigeria Phone Number Checker

Validates a Nigerian phone number's format and identifies the network operator it was originally allocated to. Everything runs locally in your browser — no number is sent anywhere.

What this does and doesn't do: it checks whether a number matches a valid Nigerian format and looks up which operator its prefix was assigned to by the NCC. It cannot and does not show a number's location, call history, or online status — that data belongs to telecom carriers and isn't available through any public tool. Because of mobile number portability, the operator shown is the original allocation and the number may since have moved to another network.
