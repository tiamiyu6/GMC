      What this scanner checks (and what it doesn't)

      Checks performed

      • HTTPS enforcement, TLS version and certificate validity
      • Security headers: HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy
      • Cookie flags: Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite
      • CORS configuration (wildcard origins combined with credentials)
      • Server/technology banner disclosure
      • Common exposed files: .env, .git, backups, credentials
      • Directory listing, mixed content, outdated front-end libraries
      • robots.txt hints and security.txt presence

      What it deliberately does not do

      • No SQL injection, XSS, or other payload injection
      • No login brute-forcing or credential attacks
      • No denial-of-service / load testing
      • No port scanning beyond the site's own web port
      • No automated exploitation of anything it finds

      Only scan sites you own or have explicit permission to test. Scanning third-party sites without authorization may be illegal in your jurisdiction, even for passive checks like these.
